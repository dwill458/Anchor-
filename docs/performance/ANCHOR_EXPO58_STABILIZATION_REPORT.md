## 7. Recommendation

Do not claim stabilization or release readiness. The corrected focused 20-sample run and profile-10 local SQLCipher fault/restart cases passed, but they do not cover Firebase auto-login or explain the original owner-data hang. Resolve the 25 classified but still failing Jest suites and determine whether that original stall can be reproduced and tied to a specific operation. Before release profiling, obtain an authorized compatible Expo 58 ARM64 artifact and measure release-like startup, Focus completion, frame pacing, memory, and recovery with a recorded sample plan. Until those blockers close, defer feature work and full-release profiling; preserve the current dirty worktree and owner data.
