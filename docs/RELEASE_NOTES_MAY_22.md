# Release Notes — May 22, 2025 and Later

---

## What's New

### Deep Prime — Immersive Audio Experience
Deep Prime sessions now include a full guided audio experience. Voice cues walk you through each phase — opening, intention-setting, deepening, visualization, and return — while an ambient drone plays softly in the background. Audio gently fades out when you exit a session so you're never jolted back.

### 15-Minute Session Option
You can now choose a 15-minute Deep Prime session. Head to Session Defaults to pick your preferred duration: 2, 5, 10, or 15 minutes.

### Breathing Animation — More Presence, More Power
The breathing animation in Deep Prime has been refined to feel more alive and intentional. The inhale, hold, and exhale rhythm is now consistent across all session types, and the visual pulse is visibly stronger to help you lock in.

### Thread Strength — See Your Progress
Tap the stats card on any Anchor's detail screen to open the new Thread Strength sheet. It shows your current streak, a week-by-week session calendar, and a full breakdown of how your connection to that anchor has grown over time.

### Rank & Depth Progression Tiers
Your growth now has more shape to it. Prime Rank and Prime Depth each have their own progression tiers with milestone tracking. Your profile card now shows pip indicators so you can see exactly where you are and what's ahead.

### Focus Session Audio Guidance
Focus sessions now include optional ambient background sounds and timed audio cues calibrated to your session length — a quieter, more grounded complement to Deep Prime.

### Set Anchor as Wallpaper
From any Anchor's detail screen, you can now set it directly as your phone wallpaper — keeping your intention visible throughout the day.

### Session History Synced Across Devices
Your session data — total activations, current streak, and week history — is now loaded from your account the moment you sign in, so your progress is always accurate no matter which device you use.

## Improvements

- **AI Variations** — Reduced from 4 to 2 per generation for faster results and a more focused selection. Visual styles for sacred geometry and gilded manuscript prompts have been upgraded for richer output.
- **Intention text in Deep Prime** uses a themed serif font that better matches the mood and depth of the session.
- **Anchor sigil** now appears in the Thread Strength sheet header instead of a generic icon.
- **Paywall login flow** now correctly returns you to your Vault after signing in.

---

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue where session data wasn't loading correctly on first sign-in.
- Fixed Vault sync not updating after mobile app re-launch.
- Resolved a settings screen crash on certain devices.
- Auth sign-in edge cases that could leave you stuck in a loading state have been resolved.
- Navigation routing after completing your first anchor now works reliably across all entry paths.
- Fixed the Progression Sheet not sliding up properly on some screen sizes.

---

## Under the Hood

- App binary size reduced — onboarding images, icons, and splash assets have been optimized and converted to WebP.
- Android app permissions have been cleaned up. Anchor no longer requests access to your camera, microphone, or media library unless those features are actively in use.
- Code security hardening: minification enabled, local caches secured, Android backup disabled to protect your data.
- Licenses screen added under Settings so you can view all open-source attributions.
