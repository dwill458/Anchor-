## Design contract

Creation retains its current state machine, timing constants, canonical distillation, Kamea computation, saved SVG, generation adapters, two-candidate validation, persistence and Home handoff. ## Changes

- Intention: larger ink writing, a single baseline, open suggestion rows and subtle static printed stipple in place of stacked cream cards.
- Distillation: survivor color follows actual room polarity; removed letters retain their cascade and measured compaction. Surviving row letters withdraw into their traveling mapping tokens instead of remaining as duplicate highlighted characters.
- Construction: subdued fine cream grid, tabular numerals, brief category numeral response, smaller points and progressively withdrawn numbers, rules, plate/registration marks and mapping points. Existing drawing and hold clocks are unchanged.
- Reveal: the same stored geometry remains visible through dark hold and the return to cream. Neutral ink bridges the contrast crossover before category color returns. Construction copy stays withdrawn until reveal copy arrives.
- Expression: larger primary-preview allocation; alternatives retain their actual treatments and selected check, with fewer card borders/shadows.
- Generation: extended visible structural retrace until the real surface sequence resolves; existing honest waiting, retry and candidate decoding contracts remain intact. Dark failure text receives a cream structural surface.
- Candidates: existing central split retained, with larger diagonal portrait compositions and side-by-side short-stage fallback. Selection responds continuously at 2.5% prominence; the existing selected-candidate resolve and Home handoff retain their geometry and clocks.
- Accessibility: OS motion preference is honored under Anchor's system setting; explicit app on/off overrides retain their existing policy. Status bar and back icon follow environmental polarity. Large survivor contrast is sampled throughout the tonal transition; small supporting copy recedes at its polarity crossover.
- Performance: fixed SVG stipple; no blur, filters, masks, particles or animated shadows added. Device frame-rate acceptance still requires measurement.
