Home teaching, rebuilt as getting-started steps

Matched to the live 2.0 screens. A getting-started card on Home teaches every path by having the user walk it once. Tips appear at the moment they apply, or when the user taps “Show me”.

Jump to a state

The six steps

  1. Do your first practice. On return, the glow fades in and a tip says it's your Thread Strength.
  2. See your progress. Before a Vision it's the Progress section on Home. After, a one-time tip says Progress moved to Thread Strength.
  3. Open your anchor. The “Tap your anchor” chip stays until they do.
  4. Add a Vision and 5. Chart your path. Both on the anchor's page; the tip points there first.
  5. Make another anchor. Show me points at +. Once there are two, a tip teaches swiping and All Anchors.

What the code does today

  • One spotlight per app session (MAX_COACH_SEQUENCES_PER_SESSION = 1, Home cap: 1).
  • Home has two beats live: home.today and home.anchor. home.progress and home.thread are retired.
  • Vision, Chart and + aren't taught anywhere. detailsTeachingBeats() returns [].
